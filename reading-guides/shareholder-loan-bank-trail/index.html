Trace a direct S corporation shareholder loan from bank to ledger

Build a transaction record that shows who advanced funds, who received them, and when.

Worked example

An owner wires $20,000 from a personal account, but the corporate ledger labels the deposit additional capital. Check the loan documents, transaction reference and approvals before classifying the advance.

What to document

Retain evidence of the actual transfer, the stated terms and the ledger entries. Have the preparer reconcile the documented debt and determine whether it supports debt basis.
