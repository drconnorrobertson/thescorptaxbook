Document a shareholder payment under an S corporation loan guarantee

Separate signing a guarantee from paying the lender under it before reviewing debt basis.

Worked example

An owner guarantees a company note for $30,000. The owner pays the lender $4,000 after a demand. Collect the guarantee, demand, proof of payment and corporate records. The guarantee alone does not establish a $30,000 debt-basis amount.

What to document

Ask the preparer how to treat the actual payment and whether it must be reconciled with amounts due to the shareholder. Keep guarantee and payment records separate.
