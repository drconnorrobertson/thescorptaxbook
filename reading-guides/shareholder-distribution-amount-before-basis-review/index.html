Prepare an S corporation distribution list before reviewing stock basis

Reconcile cash paid to the shareholder and preserve evidence for other transfers that may affect the review.

Worked example

A corporate bank account shows two $6,000 transfers and one documented $900 expense reimbursement. Match each payment to its approval and support so the preparer can classify the entries before determining the distribution amount.

What to document

Separate confirmed distributions, expense reimbursements, payroll and loan activity in the workpaper. Tie the amount used for the distribution calculation to the return and stock-basis record.
