Reconcile the opening debt-basis file before an S corporation loan repayment

Compare the loan balance and adjusted debt basis before the company repays a shareholder.

Worked example

The loan ledger shows $24,000 due. The prior Form 7203 shows that losses previously reduced the associated debt basis. Identify the adjusted basis and changes since the prior return before assuming how a repayment will be treated.

What to document

Give the preparer the note, prior Form 7203, dates and amounts advanced, and the planned repayment. Ask which items affect basis, and keep the final repayment workpaper with the lender records.
