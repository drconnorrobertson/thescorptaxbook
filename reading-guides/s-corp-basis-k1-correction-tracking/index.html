Keep a revision record when an S corporation Schedule K-1 changes

Trace corrected items to the stock and debt basis workpapers before using revised balances.

Worked example

A corrected K-1 changes the income shown for the prior year. Compare the two versions line by line, then identify which opening stock or debt basis entries and loss carryforwards rely on the changed figure.

What to document

Save both versions, the revision date and the affected worksheet lines. Have the preparer determine which periods need review and record which figures were used on any filed return.
