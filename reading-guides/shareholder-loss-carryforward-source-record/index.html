Track an S corporation loss suspended by the basis limit

Maintain the year, category and reason for each suspended amount so it can be reviewed in later years.

Worked example

A shareholder receives a $9,000 current-year loss item. The prior-year workpaper separately shows $3,000 of prior losses limited by basis. Record the $3,000 separately from any amount restricted under another rule before the new year is calculated.

What to document

Keep Form 7203 and the source K-1 with the carryforward worksheet. Have the preparer reconcile any amount released and record the year and test that allowed it.
