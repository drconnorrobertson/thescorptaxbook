How to read, verify, and use this educational library.

Facts, examples, and results

New decision guides identify illustrative situations as examples. They do not represent completed client engagements. Published client results should be read at their original AE source with the relevant facts and limitations. No result is guaranteed.

Primary guidance and applicable years

Decision guides link to relevant primary federal guidance. Source versions and applicable rules can change. Check the reporting year, effective dates, state treatment, and your circumstances with a qualified professional before implementing a strategy.

Editorial ownership

This site is owned by AE Tax Advisors. Provider-comparison material is AE-owned education, not an independent ranking. Professional qualifications should be verified with the relevant licensing authority when evaluating an engagement.

Preparation tools and privacy

Read the instructions for each tool before entering information. Use document references instead of personal identifiers in browser worksheets. Keep exported files securely and ask AE for its secure intake process before sending returns or financial records.

Questions or corrections

Contact team@aetaxadvisors.com with the page address and the specific question. Avoid including confidential tax records in a general email inquiry.
