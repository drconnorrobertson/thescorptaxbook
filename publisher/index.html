The publisher and the advisory firm

AE Tax Advisors publishes this educational resource. Its companion guides help readers organize S corporation planning questions and records. Individual advice requires a separate review of the owner’s actual facts and a scoped engagement.

Why this S corporation publication exists

An S election should fit the owner's work, income and administrative capacity. Payroll, distributions, reimbursements and shareholder basis belong in a coordinated system. Readers can use the book to understand the decision and the companion guides to prepare the records needed for implementation.

Start with reasonable compensation, then read the basis and accountable-plan examples. Coordinate benefits and retirement funding with payroll. Revisit eligibility before adding shareholders, changing economic rights or transferring ownership.

What to bring to an S corporation planning discussion

  • Election acceptance, ownership documents and recent returns.
  • The owner's duties, hours, payroll history and compensation evidence.
  • Distribution, shareholder-loan and basis schedules.
  • Reimbursement records, benefit arrangements and retirement documents.
